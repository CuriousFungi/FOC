================================================================================
MONITORING CHECKLIST
================================================================================

When monitoring blend transition, verify:

Essential Variables:
☐ g_hybrid_blend_factor (0.0 → 1.0 smoothly)
☐ g_hybrid_measured_speed (increases smoothly)
☐ g_kalman_velocity (matches measured_speed)

Angle Variables:
☐ g_open_loop_elec_before_blend (sawtooth pattern)
☐ g_closed_loop_elec_before_blend (sawtooth pattern)
☐ g_blended_angle_before_rate_limit (smooth transition)
☐ Difference between open/closed < 0.5 rad before transition

Duty Cycle Variables:
☐ g_dutycycle_1A, 1B, 2A, 2b (all smooth sinusoids)
☐ No glitches or discontinuities during transition
☐ Amplitude remains stable

Quality Checks:
☐ No oscillations in blend_factor
☐ Transition occurs in expected speed range (10-20 rad/s)
☐ Motor runs smoothly (no audible noise or stutter)
☐ Encoder angle increases continuously

================================================================================
SUMMARY
================================================================================

GOOD TRANSITION:
✓ Blend factor: smooth 0.0 → 1.0 ramp
✓ Speed: steady increase through thresholds
✓ Angles: track together, no jumps
✓ Duty cycles: smooth throughout
✓ Motor: quiet, smooth operation

BAD TRANSITION:
✗ Blend factor: oscillating or stuck
✗ Speed: erratic or not reaching threshold
✗ Angles: discontinuities or large differences
✗ Duty cycles: glitches or distortion
✗ Motor: stuttering, noise, vibration

ADD TO YOUR MONITOR: g_hybrid_blend_factor
WATCH THE MAGIC: 0.0 → 1.0 smooth transition! ✨
